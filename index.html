About R&C Albion

R&C Albion (Refining Calculator) is a high-performance web tool built specifically for Albion Online players. Our mission is to take the guesswork out of market flipping and manufacturing.

Whether you are calculating Resource Return Rates (RRR) in Royal City refining bonuses, factoring in station usage fees, or tracking your exact Return on Investment (ROI), R&C Albion provides lightning-fast accuracy to maximize your silver profits.

This tool is actively maintained. Found a bug or have a suggestion? Use the "Report a Bug" link in the footer — every report is reviewed.
